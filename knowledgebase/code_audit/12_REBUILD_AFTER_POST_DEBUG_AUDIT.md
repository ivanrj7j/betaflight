### 3-Part Deep-Dive Critique

#### What Went Wrong
1. **Debug Scaffolding Spread Across Upstream Core Files:** During rapid silicon hypothesis testing, `aries_probe.h` calls were spliced into `cli.c`, `tasks.c`, and `msp_serial.c`, and `startup.S` kept an `#if ARIES_PROBE_DEBUG` branch that behaves differently (`probeTrapMode = 0`) from release builds.
2. **Long-Lived Diagnostic Artifacts Stayed Unpruned:** `bootstrap_diag.c` (`1,495` lines) stayed in `src/platform/ARIES/` long after full `TARGET=ARIESV3` superseded it, and `millis()`'s `carry * 67296U + lo_sum` 32-bit overflow remained untouched from earlier phases.

#### What Could Have Been Done Better
1. **Separate Step D5 Cleanup Before Advancing Branches:** Running `H1_DIV_ATOM`/`H5_DIV_ATOM` once under `ARIES_PROBE_DEBUG=1` and immediately stripping `aries_probe.{c,h}` in a dedicated commit would have kept `src/main/` diffs minimal before starting `rebuild-after`.
2. **Make Verification Scripts Branch-Family Aware:** Checking `master` exclusion or `rebuild*` prefix in `build_verify.sh` avoids breaking the 10-point audit whenever a follow-up working branch is created.

#### What Were the Good / Brilliant Parts
1. **Clean Gating Kept Production `.bin` Small:** Guarding `aries_probe.c` under `#if ARIES_PROBE_DEBUG > 0` kept the production image (`194,932 B` `.bin`, `_end` well within `242 KB`) clean enough to verify a `5m49s` (`13,328` TX / `9,118` RX MSP) Configurator soak on physical silicon without drops.
2. **Exact Errata Isolation & Unified `platform.h` Primitives:** Consolidating all `BASEPRI` and atomic helpers around `thejas32_atomic_enter()` / `thejas32_atomic_exit()` eliminated duplicated raw CSR inline assembly and fixed `Erratum 13` across every caller at once.
